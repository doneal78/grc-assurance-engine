OBSERVED · LOCAL REGISTER · DEFINITION {{ observed.definition_version }}

Measured from saved records

These counts come from the local SQLite register. Assessment volume is how many assessments are saved. Persisted dispositions are the human decisions saved on those assessments (approve, approve with conditions, escalate, or reject). A public /try/ run that is not saved is in neither number, and it is not a register disposition. Self-reported human handling time is optional minutes entered on a decision. Self-reported rework time is a separate optional number for time spent redoing work after a first pass. Each is shown when at least one decision has that number. Neither is elapsed cycle time, and neither is the modeled panel below. An empty register shows zeros. Definitions, rounding, and unmeasured quantities.
