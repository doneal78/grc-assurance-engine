Vendor and AI risk, with a visible evidence trail.

A small, rules-based workflow for turning assessment inputs and evidence into reviewable results.

What this demonstrates

  • Evidence-first third-party and AI vendor risk review.
  • Explainable, versioned rules. The same inputs and the same rule-set version produce the same result.
  • Draft NIST, ISO/IEC 27001, and ISO/IEC 42001 control mapping. The ISO entries stay draft.
  • Human-in-the-loop decisions with a tamper-evident (SHA-256 hash chain) audit trail.
Illustrative model · Synthetic scenarios · Human review required

Risk rules and mappings are draft portfolio examples. Results are not approvals, certifications, or proof of control effectiveness.
