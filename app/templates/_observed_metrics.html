Self-reported handling minutes are the numbers a person typed for review time. Self-reported rework minutes are the numbers a person typed for time spent redoing work after a first pass. Self-reported manual touches are the whole-number count a person typed for human touchpoints on that assessment. Self-reported handoffs are the whole-number count a person typed for times the assessment was handed between people or queues before disposition. Authenticated effort is a separate optional number of minutes a signed-in reviewer entered on a disposition. The application does not time the session. Elapsed cycle time is the clock span between two workflow events. None of these numbers is an input to the modeled cost panel. The four self-reported figures have no identity check and no timesheet. Authenticated effort is collected only after the demo password gate. It is not a per-person identity system.
