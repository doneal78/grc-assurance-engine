Definitions (v{{ observed.definition_version }})
Full wording for definition {{ observed.definition_version }}. The numbers above use these rules. They are not calculate_cost().
FigureWording
What the cards count These counts come from the SQLite register on this host. Assessment volume is how many assessments are saved. Persisted dispositions are the human decisions saved on those assessments (approve, approve with conditions, escalate, or reject). A public /try/ run that is not saved is in neither number, and it is not a register disposition. Self-reported human handling time is optional minutes entered on a decision. Self-reported rework time is a separate optional number for time spent redoing work after a first pass. Self-reported manual touches are a separate optional count of human touchpoints on that assessment. Self-reported handoffs are a separate optional count of times the assessment was handed between people or queues before disposition. Each is shown when at least one decision has that number. Monthly assessment rate is a separate count of current decisions whose timestamp falls in the 30 days ending at the latest readable decision. It is shown only when those timestamps cover at least 30 days. A shorter history says Not measured. Authenticated effort is a separate optional number of minutes a signed-in reviewer entered on a disposition. It is shown when at least one decision has that number. A public /try/ save does not collect it. It is not a session timer. Cash savings are a separate optional number of whole dollars a signed-in reviewer entered when a spending reduction was known. The card shows the median when at least one decision has that number, and Not measured when none do. A public /try/ save does not collect them. They are not a wage times the other figures. Avoided-incident value is a separate modeled-assumption card. It says Not measured while incident_cost and avoided_incident_count are null. It is not an observed register figure, not cash savings, and not the labor-capacity panel. None of these figures is the modeled panel below. An empty register shows zeros for the counts that are counts. These counts are definition {{ observed.definition_version }} applied to this browser's sandbox plus any saved row that has no sandbox id. Another browser's sandbox is not included. They are not calculate_cost(). A scope with no saved decision still says Not measured.

How the typed numbers differ Self-reported handling minutes are the numbers a person typed for review time. Self-reported rework minutes are the numbers a person typed for time spent redoing work after a first pass. Self-reported manual touches are the whole-number count a person typed for human touchpoints on that assessment. Self-reported handoffs are the whole-number count a person typed for times the assessment was handed between people or queues before disposition. Authenticated effort is a separate optional number of minutes a signed-in reviewer entered on a disposition. The application does not time the session. Cash savings are a separate optional number of whole dollars a signed-in reviewer entered when a spending reduction was known. The application does not multiply the other figures by a wage. Elapsed cycle time is the clock span between two workflow events. None of these numbers is an input to the modeled cost panel. The four self-reported figures have no identity check and no timesheet. Authenticated effort and cash savings are collected only after the demo password gate. Neither is a per-person identity system, and the dollars are not checked against a ledger.
